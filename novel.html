NOVEL

A Craftsman's Journey

The novel follows a man preparing for a move that stirs memories he has spent a lifetime avoiding. As he reflects on his past, he is forced to confront the childhood abandonment that shaped his relationships, choices, and sense of self, leading to a long-overdue reckoning with the wounds he has carried for decades.

Publication Status

A Craftsman's Journey is currently in professional editing and publication preparation, with an anticipated release in Winter 2027.
