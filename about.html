ABOUT

Curiosity is at the center of everything I write.

I'm fascinated by people, the choices they make, and the ways a life can change course. Stories often begin with something familiar, but they rarely stay there. I'm interested in what happened, what could have happened, and the places life might have gone if we had trusted ourselves a little more or stopped fighting what was already unfolding around us.

Through fiction, I follow questions rather than answers. Some stories emerge from real experiences. Others grow from observation, imagination, or a simple curiosity about where a particular path might lead.

Above all, I write stories about people and possibility.
